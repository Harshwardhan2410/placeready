Interview Tips

Tap a question to expand.

How do I answer "Tell me about yourself"?

Use a 60-second structure: present (course, skills), past (projects, internships), future (why this role). Avoid reciting your resume.

How should I prepare for coding rounds?

Master arrays, strings, hashing, recursion and basic DP. Solve 2 problems daily and practice explaining your approach aloud.

What should my resume contain?

One page, 2–3 strong projects with measurable outcomes, skills, and links to GitHub or portfolio. Keep formatting clean.

How do I handle a question I don't know?

Stay calm, think aloud, state what you do know, and reason towards an answer. Honesty beats bluffing.
